How location classification works

Step 1 — Indian check
Location or company contains an Indian hint → classified as India → counted against India cap.
Step 2 — Country inference
If not Indian, location string is matched against a country alias map (US, UK, Germany, etc.).
Step 3 — Offshore cap
Offshore jobs are capped globally and per-country to avoid any one country dominating results.
